About Sustainable Cloths

Clothing the way it used to be made.

We work directly with handloom weaver families, who turn natural yarn into fabric one thread at a time. Every garment we sell, for women and men, comes from this work.

We use only cotton, linen, pure silk, hemp, Tencel, wool, rayon and pure georgette. We never use polyester, nylon or any plastic-based material, in the cloth, the thread or the packaging.

Woven by hand

No power looms. Small colour and weave variations show your piece was made by a person.

Natural fibres only

Breathable, long-lasting and biodegradable at the end of its life.

Fair to weavers

Weavers are paid fairly for each piece, so the craft can continue.

How do I care for handloom clothes?

Wash gently in cold water with mild soap. Dry in shade. Silk and wool are best dry-cleaned.

Why do colours look slightly different?

Natural fibres and hand weaving mean each piece is unique, and screens vary.
